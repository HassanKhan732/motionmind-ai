# MotionMind AI - Pakistani Sign Language (PSL) Curriculum

> Educational curriculum designed to help children and educators learn and practice PSL using computer vision.

| Level | Sign (Urdu) | English Meaning | Category | Hand Shape & Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **السلام علیکم (Salaam)** | Peace / Hello | Greeting | Flat open hand raised near temple/forehead, fingertips touching forehead gently |
| 2 | **شکریہ (Shukriya)** | Thank You | Etiquette | Fingertips touching the chin, then moving gently outward toward the listener |
| 3 | **ہاں (Haan)** | Yes | Response | Fist with thumb tucked, nodding motion downwards like a head nod |
| 3 | **نہیں (Nahi)** | No | Response | Index finger extended and waving side to side horizontally |
| 4 | **پانی (Paani)** | Water | Basic Needs | Three middle fingers extended (W shape) near mouth |
| 5 | **مدد (Madad)** | Help | Emergency / Needs | Closed fist on one hand placed on the open palm of the other hand, lifted upward |
